# Data and Integrity Policy

- The supplied course data and templates may be shared with members of the registered project team for course work.
- Do not upload raw course data or templates to a public repository unless the teaching team explicitly permits it.
- Keep passwords, Canvas sessions, Kaggle credentials, API tokens, and personal identifiers out of this folder.
- Do not edit `data/raw/`.
- Record the origin and retrieval date of every external dataset.
- Canvas explicitly allows teams to collect additional data when it may help, but doing so is optional rather than a project requirement.
- Prefer authoritative, publicly accessible sources with stable identifiers and clear time coverage; document the download URL, retrieval date, licence/terms, join key, and transformations.
- External features must be available as of the prediction date; future information must not be used.
- Do not use any external source that directly or indirectly reveals the hidden test-set rent, including later releases of the same HDB rental records.
- Do not reconstruct hidden competition labels from newly published HDB records.
- Acknowledge AI assistance according to course policy and any later project-specific instructions.
